## Understanding Plant Nutrition — Industry Knowledge

### The Big Three: N, P, K

**Nitrogen (N)** — The growth nutrient. Drives leafy, green vegetative growth. Deficiency shows as yellowing starting from old (lower) leaves, pale colour, stunted growth. Excess causes lush soft growth susceptible to pests.

**Phosphorus (P)** — The root and establishment nutrient. Critical for root development, energy transfer, flowering and seed production. Most important at planting and establishment. Deficiency shows as purpling of leaves, poor root development, delayed maturity.

**Potassium (K)** — The strength and stress nutrient. Improves drought tolerance, fruit quality, disease resistance and water regulation. Deficiency shows as brown leaf edges (scorch), poor fruit quality, weak stems.

### Trace Elements

Essential in small quantities but just as important as NPK:
- **Iron (Fe)** — Required for chlorophyll production. Deficiency causes yellowing between leaf veins (interveinal chlorosis) on young leaves. Common in high pH (alkaline) soils.
- **Zinc (Zn)** — Important for enzyme function and growth regulation. Deficiency causes small, distorted leaves, short internodes, poor fruit set.
- **Manganese (Mn)** — Involved in photosynthesis. Deficiency similar to iron — yellowing between veins.
- **Boron (B)** — Critical for pollination and fruit set. Deficiency causes hollow fruit, poor set, distorted new growth.
- **Copper (Cu)** — Enzyme function, disease resistance. Deficiency causes blue-green leaf colour, dieback.
- **Calcium (Ca)** — Cell wall strength, fruit quality. Deficiency causes blossom end rot in tomatoes, bitter pit in apples, tip burn in lettuce.
- **Magnesium (Mg)** — Central to chlorophyll. Deficiency causes interveinal yellowing on older leaves first.
- **Sulphur (S)** — Protein synthesis, enzyme function. Deficiency causes uniform yellowing of young leaves.
- **Silicon (Si)** — Strengthens cell walls, improves pest and disease resistance, drought tolerance. Not always listed as essential but increasingly recognised as highly beneficial, especially in Australian soils.

### Understanding Soil pH

pH measures how acidic or alkaline your soil is on a scale of 0–14. Most plants prefer 6.0–7.0.

- **Acidic soil (below 6.0):** Aluminium and manganese become toxic; phosphorus, calcium and magnesium become less available. Common in high-rainfall coastal areas of Australia.
- **Neutral soil (6.0–7.0):** Most nutrients available. Ideal for most plants.
- **Alkaline soil (above 7.0):** Iron, zinc, manganese and boron become locked up. Common in arid and semi-arid areas of Australia, and in soils with limestone or heavy irrigation.

Signs of pH problems: Plants don't respond to fertiliser, yellowing despite feeding, stunted growth.

### Soil Biology

Soil is alive. A healthy teaspoon of soil contains billions of microorganisms — bacteria, fungi, protozoa — that break down organic matter, fix nitrogen, and make nutrients available to plants. Healthy soil biology is the foundation of plant health.

What kills soil biology: Synthetic herbicides and pesticides, excessive tillage, leaving soil bare, waterlogging, compaction.

What builds soil biology: Organic matter (compost, mulch), humic and fulvic acids (Humic Carbon Liquid), silica (SilicaMax), minimal disturbance, keeping soil covered.

## Common Australian Plant Problems — Diagnosis Guide

### Yellow Leaves

- **Uniform yellowing, older leaves first** → Nitrogen deficiency → Pro-Strength Lawn Fertiliser (lawns) or Liquid NPK / Premium GP (gardens)
- **Yellowing between veins, young leaves** → Iron or manganese deficiency → EasyTrace foliar spray
- **Yellowing between veins, older leaves** → Magnesium deficiency → CalMag 51
- **Pale overall, purpling underneath** → Phosphorus deficiency → Premium GP Garden Fertiliser
- **Yellow with brown edges** → Potassium deficiency or drought stress → Liquid NPK with high K, water management
- **Yellow patches in lawn** → Often iron or trace element deficiency → EasyTrace

### Poor Growth

- Slow growth despite feeding → Check soil pH, consider Humic Carbon Liquid to improve nutrient availability
- Stunted seedlings → SeedPrimer at next sowing, or Eco-Spray to stimulate establishment
- Plants wilting despite watering → May be root rot (waterlogging) or clay soil — Clay Breaker, improve drainage

### Pests and Disease

- **Soft plants attract more pests** — Silicon strengthens cell walls, making plants physically harder for insects and fungi to attack → SilicaMax or UltraSilica Wettable
- **Powdery mildew, black spot, botrytis** → Improve airflow, apply SilicaMax preventatively
- **General insect pressure (aphids, mites, whitefly)** → Eco-Spray is safe and effective for home gardens
- **Stressed plants are more susceptible** — nutrition is the first line of defence

### Lawn Problems

- Pale/yellow lawn → Pro-Strength Lawn Fertiliser, then EasyTrace if yellowing persists
- Thin or bare patches → Aerate, fertilise with Pro-Strength, oversow bare areas
- Thatchy, soft lawn → SilicaMax builds turf density and upright growth
- Lawn not recovering after winter → Pro-Strength in early spring after aerating
- Lawn in heavy clay → Clay Breaker liquid to improve drainage and root penetration

### Fruiting Problems

- Flowers dropping without setting fruit → BLOOM-n-YIELD, check boron levels
- Small or few fruit → BLOOM-n-YIELD at bud stage
- Poor fruit colour → Colour Spray + NPK LeafSpray Plus pre-harvest
- Blossom end rot (tomatoes, capsicum) → CalMag 51 — calcium deficiency
- Low sugar/bland taste → Booster during fruit fill

### Soil Problems

- Heavy clay, waterlogging → Clay Breaker Powder or Clay Breaker Liquid
- Sandy soil, nutrients leaching → Humic Carbon Liquid to improve nutrient retention
- Depleted, tired soil → Humic Carbon Liquid + SilicaMax together to rebuild biology
- Soil not responding to fertiliser → Check pH, consider Trace Elements Volcanic to rebuild mineral profile

## Australian Climate and Growing Conditions

Australia has highly variable climate zones. Key considerations for plant nutrition:

**Australian soils are generally:**
- Ancient and highly weathered — low in trace elements and organic matter
- Often acidic in high-rainfall coastal areas (NSW, Qld coast, WA south-west)
- Alkaline in inland and arid areas
- Low in silica in plant-available form due to millions of years of leaching
- Often deficient in zinc, iron, manganese, boron and copper

**Heat and drought stress:**
- Summer heat causes rapid nutrient leaching from sandy soils
- Potassium (K) is critical for heat and drought tolerance
- Silicon (SilicaMax) significantly improves drought tolerance by reducing water loss through leaves
- Calcium (CalMag 51) helps cells withstand heat damage

**Seasonal timing (Southern Australia):**
- Spring (Sept–Nov): Main growth flush — apply fertiliser as growth begins. Best time for lawn fertilising.
- Summer (Dec–Feb): Stress period — focus on potassium, silica, irrigation management
- Autumn (Mar–May): Second growth flush — good time for soil conditioners and building for winter
- Winter (Jun–Aug): Dormancy — minimal feeding for most plants; prepare soil for spring

**Seasonal timing (Northern Australia / Tropics):**
- Wet season (Oct–Apr): Main growing season — apply fertilisers, expect leaching on sandy soils
- Dry season (May–Sept): Dormancy or slow growth — focus on soil improvement

## How to Answer Common Customer Questions

**"My plants look sick but I don't know why"**
Start with Eco-Spray as an all-in-one response — it feeds, stimulates and helps with pest/disease resistance. Also recommend SilicaMax to build long-term plant strength.

**"What do I put on my veggie garden?"**
Premium GP Garden Fertiliser monthly, BLOOM-n-YIELD when flowering starts, CalMag 51 if growing tomatoes or capsicum.

**"My lawn is yellow"**
Pro-Strength Lawn Fertiliser first. If yellow patches persist after 10 days, add EasyTrace for trace element correction.

**"How do I improve my soil?"**
Clay soils: Clay Breaker. Sandy soils: Humic Carbon Liquid. All soils: SilicaMax activates biology. Depleted soils: Trace Elements Volcanic rebuilds mineral profile.

**"I've never gardened before — where do I start?"**
Eco-Spray is the easiest starting point — one product that does everything. Premium GP Garden Fertiliser for garden beds. Tell them gardening is simpler than they think.

**"Why aren't my fruit trees producing?"**
BLOOM-n-YIELD at bud formation. Check for boron deficiency (poor pollination). Booster during fruit fill for size and colour.

**"My seeds won't germinate"**
SeedPrimer at next sowing. Check soil temperature (most seeds need 15°C+). Don't overwater — seeds rot in waterlogged soil.
